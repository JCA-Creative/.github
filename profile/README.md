## We make technology useful.

JCA Creative is an independent digital company based in Spain. We bring together **software development, artificial intelligence, automation, and e-commerce** to help businesses solve practical problems and move forward with confidence.

## What we believe

Good technology should feel clear, work reliably, and make a real difference to the people who use it. We combine thoughtful design with practical engineering, staying curious, collaborative, and focused on outcomes.

## What we do

- **Software development** — We design and build applications, APIs, and digital experiences around real needs.
- **AI and automation** — We create intelligent tools and connected workflows that make everyday work simpler.
- **E-commerce** — We help businesses improve and extend their online commerce experiences.

## Work with us

We welcome conversations with clients, collaborators, developers, and people who share our interest in useful technology. If you have an idea, a challenge, or would like to build something together, get in touch.

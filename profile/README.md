## We build useful technology for modern businesses.

JCA Creative is a Spain-based digital company building across **software development, AI tools, automation, and e-commerce**. We turn real business needs into useful digital products and connected systems.

## What we do

| Software development | AI tools |
|:--|:--|
| Custom applications, APIs, and digital products designed around practical business needs. | AI-powered products and workflows for content creation, productivity, and business operations. |
| **Automation** | **E-commerce** |
| Connected workflows that reduce repetitive work and keep business systems in sync. | Shopify-focused tools and digital experiences that support online stores. |

## Products & projects

### JCA.AI — AI video advertising
A product-to-video platform that helps e-commerce teams turn product details into UGC-style video ads. The product includes a creator-facing web app, a core API for accounts and AI generation, and an operations console for managing platform activity.

### JCA Mail — email marketing
An email campaign platform for e-commerce businesses, bringing campaign management, delivery workflows, automation, and performance reporting together.

### JCA Pay — Shopify checkout
A multi-store Shopify checkout API for custom cash-on-delivery experiences, with server-verified pricing, discounts, order creation, and optional Meta Conversions API reporting.

We care about clear experiences, reliable engineering, and technology that solves practical problems. From early ideas to production-ready products, we build systems that make digital work simpler and more useful.
